Speech-act gap graph

Where the learner-pragmatics literature in your coding sheet is missing papers. Pick any pair of dimensions (region, speech act, method, period, country and more) to see which combinations are absent or thin compared with what the rest of the field predicts. Red marks a gap.

Line thickness is the number of papers. Numbers on boxes count paper links in this view, so a paper that names two acts counts twice. Select a box or a line for the papers behind it.

Drag the map sideways, or use Zoom to for a region.
