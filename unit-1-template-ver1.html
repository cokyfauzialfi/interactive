Adab Discussion Prompt

If Dr. Hakim's actual intention was to withhold clear permission, what would a response closer to qaulan sadida (clear, correct, truthful speech) have looked like, without losing courtesy?

Key insight: Politeness and clarity are not opposites. A courteous reply can still be unambiguous, and an unclear reply is not made more respectful by being softened — it simply relocates the misunderstanding.

Concept Input

Core ideas using the Snapshot as a running illustration

🔤

3.1 Semantic vs. Pragmatic Meaning

What words mean vs. what speakers mean

+

Semantics asks what a sentence means in isolation. Pragmatics asks what a speaker means by using that sentence, here, now, with this hearer.

"Bisa aja sih" has one semantic meaning but at least two live pragmatic readings: it can function as permission or as a non-committal hedge.

This is why the same sentence can lead to completely different actions — Rani submitted her assignment, while Dr. Hakim expected a follow-up question.

🧩

3.2 The Inferential Model

Communication as active inference, not code transmission

+

Communication is not simply encoding and decoding a fixed message (the "code model"). Hearers actively infer speaker meaning from:

  • What is said
  • What is left unsaid
  • What they assume about the speaker's intentions
Rani's misreading was not a comprehension failure — it was a reasonable inference given the available cues. In Indonesian academic norms, a non-refusal from a lecturer often reads as consent.
📱

3.3 Digital Communication as Pragmatic Space

What makes digital environments distinct

+

Digital communication has unique features that shape pragmatic interpretation:

🔇
Reduced Paralinguistic Cues
No tone of voice, facial expression, or pause length to disambiguate a hedge
⏳
Asynchronicity
An 11-minute reply gap can itself carry meaning — hesitation? busy? considering?
🌐
Context Collapse
The same message may be read by an audience the sender didn't anticipate
😊
New Disambiguating Resources
Emoji, punctuation, read receipts, and typing indicators partly substitute for lost cues

📝 Note on Framework Provenance

The pairing of pragmatic constructs with the six qaulan (Qur'anic speech) principles is an original integrative device proposed for this module — it is not part of the official Kurikulum Berbasis Cinta document, which centers on the five Panca Cinta pillars. Both layers are shown so the mapping's provenance stays clear to readers and reviewers.

📋 Facilitator Notes

  • The Snapshot example is intentionally mundane — almost every student will have a version of this exact exchange. Resist the temptation to pick a more dramatic misunderstanding; the ordinariness is what makes the pragmatic point land.
  • Table 1 (Framework Map) will feel abstract on first exposure. Do not over-explain it in Unit 1 — its purpose is recognition ("oh, that's what Unit 6 will be about") rather than mastery. Revisit it briefly at the start of each subsequent unit.
  • If students disagree about what Dr. Hakim "really" meant, that disagreement is the lesson, not a problem to resolve — pragmatic inference is probabilistic, not a matter of finding the one correct reading.
  • Watch for students conflating "formal" with "polite" and "casual" with "impolite" in Part 4 — the friend register is not impolite, it is differently polite. This distinction matters for Unit 6.
